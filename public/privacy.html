Privacy Policy

Eternity, from Eternity Works. Last updated October 16, 2026.

The short version. Your chats, memory and receipts are encrypted on your phone. Our server passes your messages to the AI and does not keep them. We don't sell your data, we don't use it to train AI, and ads are never personalized. You can erase everything from inside the app.

Who we are

Eternity is made by Eternity Works. When this page says "we", "us" or "Eternity", that's who it means. You can reach us at eternityworks2026@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your Crew, chats, memory, Charter, receipts and settings are stored on your device. They are encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone). We don't have that key. Your recovery key, shown in the app under Trust & privacy, is that same key, so keep it somewhere safe.

What we store on our servers

  • Your account. If you sign in, Firebase (Google) keeps your sign-in details, such as your email address or a passkey.
  • Your plan and usage. Your subscription level and a count of messages used, so we can apply plan limits.
  • Connection tokens. If you connect an app (for example GitHub or Notion), the sign-in token or key you give us is stored encrypted on our server. The app itself can never read it back. Disconnecting deletes it.

We do not store the content of your conversations on our servers.

How your messages reach the AI

When you talk to a teammate, your message travels through our server to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, and the reply comes back. We don't write your messages to our database or logs. Anthropic processes them to produce a reply. Anthropic's commercial terms say that data sent through its API is not used to train its models. When a teammate reads something from a connected app to help you, that information is sent the same way, only for that request.

Connected apps

When you connect an app, you choose to give Eternity access, and you can remove it at any time in the app or in that service's own settings. Reads happen automatically for the access you allowed. Anything that sends, posts, spends, merges or deletes always asks you first.

Eternity's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use that information only to provide the features you see in Eternity. We don't sell it, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Things your phone may ask permission for

  • Location, while you're using the app. To work out real leave-by times and local news. It isn't kept.
  • Calendar. To let your Crew plan around what's already on it. Calendar data stays on your phone unless you ask a teammate to use it.
  • Face ID or Touch ID. Used on your device only, to confirm important actions. We never receive your biometric data.
  • Notifications. To tell you when something needs you.

You can change any of these in your phone's settings at any time.

Ads

Free plans show one small ad, served by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only. We don't give ad networks your chats, Crew or interests. Ad networks may still collect basic device information, such as your IP address and device type, to deliver and measure ads.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, Google or our web checkout, and managed with RevenueCat. We never see your card number. RevenueCat tells us which plan you have.

The services we use

To run Eternity we rely on: Firebase and Google Cloud (accounts, hosting and server functions), Anthropic (the AI), RevenueCat (subscriptions) and Google AdMob (ads). Each has its own privacy policy. We share with them only what's needed for the job described above.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't use your conversations or connected data to train AI.
  • We don't build advertising profiles of you.

Your choices

  • Erase everything. In the app, go to Trust & privacy and use Forget everything. It deletes your Crew, chats, memory and receipts from your phone and deletes your account. It can't be undone.
  • Disconnect an app. Do it from the app's connection page. The stored token is deleted.
  • Private session. Talk without anything being saved or remembered.
  • Ask us. Email us to access, correct or delete anything we hold about you.

Children

Eternity is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
